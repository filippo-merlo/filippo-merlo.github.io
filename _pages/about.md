I am a researcher in Natural Language Processing and Vision-Language Models. I am currently a PhD candidate at the **Foundations of computational linguistics Lab (colab) at the University of Potsdam**, under the supervision of **Professor David Schlangen**, where I work on linguistic variation and LLMs interpretability.

## Prior Work

- Evaluating the visual scene comprehension abilities of state-of-the-art VLMs in a reference expression generation task, curating a semantically minimal COCO-based dataset and applying attention analysis to interpret model behavior *(M.Sc. thesis, Utrecht University, under Prof. Albert Gatt)*
- Investigating human-inspired progressive alignment and comparative learning frameworks for compositional concept acquisition in VLMs, presented at the **CORE Workshop 2024** at Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF)
- Exploring ChatGPT's information-seeking strategies in the 20-Questions game, which earned the **Best Evaluation Paper award at INLG-2023** *(University of Trento, under Prof. Raffaella Bernardi)*
